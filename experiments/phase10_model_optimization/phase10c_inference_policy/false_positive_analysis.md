# RoadSentinel Phase 10C — False Positive Analysis

## Executive Summary

Phase 10C systematically compares four operational inference policies on the 50-epoch YOLO11m champion across two distinct cohorts:
1. **Cohort 1 (Pilot):** 300 images with dense urban pedestrian traffic (901 small VRUs, 1,740 total VRUs).
2. **Cohort 2 (Generalization Holdout):** 300 non-pilot validation images drawn deterministically from `bdd100k_dataset/val` (151 small VRUs, 367 total VRUs).

This document analyzes the false positive (FP) dynamics of **Config A** (Flat 0.25), **Config B** (Adaptive VRU 0.20), **Config C** (Active Foveation at 0.25), and **Config D** (Adaptive Thresholding + Foveation at 0.20).

## 3. Detection Attribution: Provenance of False Positives

In the Active Foveation architectures (Config C & D), detections can originate from either the full-frame pass (`full_frame`) or the high-resolution crop fusion pass (`fused`):

### Pilot Cohort Attribution
- **Config A & B (Full-Frame Only):**
  - Config A: 263 matched full-frame FPs.
  - Config B: 371 matched full-frame FPs (all 108 new FPs originate from lowered thresholding on full-frame features).
- **Config C (Active Foveation 0.25):**
  - Full-frame FPs: **173**
  - Fused-crop FPs: **238** (57.9% of all FPs in Foveation are generated or amplified by high-resolution crop analysis).
- **Config D (Adaptive + Foveation):**
  - Full-frame FPs: **257**
  - Fused-crop FPs: **300** (53.8% from crop fusion).

### Generalization Cohort Attribution
- **Config A & B (Full-Frame Only):**
  - Config A: 55 full-frame FPs.
  - Config B: 77 full-frame FPs (+22 FPs from $[0.20, 0.25)$ threshold band).
- **Config C (Active Foveation 0.25):**
  - Full-frame FPs: **43**
  - Fused-crop FPs: **59** (57.8% of FPs generated by crop fusion).
- **Config D (Adaptive + Foveation):**
  - Full-frame FPs: **62**
  - Fused-crop FPs: **68** (52.3% from crop fusion).

---

## 4. Key Findings

1. **Precision Degradation Under Foveation:**
   - On the Generalization Cohort, Active Foveation (Config C) causes VRU precision to drop to **69.25%**, falling below the $70.0\%$ operational safety threshold.
   - High-resolution zooming on background artifacts (tree trunks, vertical street fixtures, reflections) causes the detector to hallucinate pedestrians with confidences $\ge 0.50$ (22 high-confidence FPs on Generalization Cohort, vs only 10 in full-frame).
2. **Adaptive Thresholding Retains Safety Margins:**
   - On both cohorts, Config B maintains VRU precision well above $70.0\%$:
     * Pilot: **72.86%**
     * Generalization: **74.07%**
   - The extra false positives in Config B are strictly bounded to the marginal confidence band $[0.20, 0.25)$ and do NOT inject spurious high-confidence phantom detections.
3. **Combination (Config D) Fails Precision Requirement:**
   - Combining adaptive thresholding with active foveation results in an unacceptable precision collapse (**66.65%** Pilot, **64.89%** Generalization). Config D cannot be recommended for production deployment.
